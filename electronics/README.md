# Electronics

This directory contains the planned electrical design for the STM32 Desktop Robotic Manipulator.

## Servo Power Distribution

The schematic illustrates the planned power distribution for the servo motors and the control system.

The servos will be powered by a dedicated 5 V, 20 A switching power supply, separate from the USB power supplied to the STM32 Nucleo board.

The STM32 and servo power system will share a common ground. The final wiring, fuse rating and connector selection will be verified before assembly.

**Note:** This is a preliminary design and may be updated during development.
